# Decided to sell? 3 steps to your listing price (IG + TikTok). Checked 6 Nov 2026

## Instagram
Ready to sell your condo, but don't know where to start? Here are 3 steps to your listing price.

Step 1: your real value. Sales in your stack, your floor range and your size. In one D13 condo, the same 1-bedder sold anywhere from $840k to $965k in 2 years, so the condo average tells you little.
Step 2: your competition. Units for sale now, their asking prices, and how fast they sell. Buyers compare you with these.
Step 3: your price. Your real value plus today's market, with some room to negotiate. Then list once, at the right price.

My take: too high and your listing goes stale. Too low and you leave money on the table. Get the numbers first.
