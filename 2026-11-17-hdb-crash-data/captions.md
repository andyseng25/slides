19,500 HDB flats hit MOP in 2028. Sure crash, right?

Not so fast. In 2021, even more flats hit MOP (25,530). Prices went up 12.7%.

I checked 36 years of HDB prices. Only 2 real crashes:
1996 to 2002: down 30%
2013 to 2019: down 12%

Both times, the govt tightened loans AND there were too many new flats. Today, only one of those is true. The govt is easing, jobs are strong, loan rates are low.

My read: HDB prices are not set up to crash. If curbs or job losses come back, I'll tell you.
